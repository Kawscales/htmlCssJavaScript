Weapons Locations

Dual Katana

Location: Hanbei's House on Mount Yōtei in the Yōtei Grasslands. Inspect the katana inside his house to trigger the cutscene, accept Hanbei's quest, and unlock the weapon.

Odachi

Location: Travel to Hurnai's Rest Inn in the Tokachi Range to find Master Yoshida.

Yari Spear

Location: Unlocked upon reaching the Ishikari Plains and following local master quests.

Kusarigama

Location: Travel to Owl Mountain in the northern Teshio Ridge region. Meet Master Enomoto and complete his questline to unlock the Kusarigama.

Ranged & Firearms

Location: There are 3 ranged weapons you can get: the Hankyu (Bow) and Yumi (Longbow) can both be purchased at any Inn. The Matchlock Rifle (Musket) automatically unlocks during the main story during the Spider and Dragon fight.

I hope this guide helps you find and unlock all the weapons!
