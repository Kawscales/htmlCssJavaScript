Weapon Alignment & Counters

Katana / Wolf Blade

Effective Against: Katana Wielders.

Strategy: Your default balanced weapon. Ideal for 1v1 duels against enemy swordsmen using quick light attacks and timed parries.

Dual Katana

Effective Against: Polearms & Spears (Yari/Naginata).

Strategy: Delivers rapid strikes that slice through longer two-handed weapons before spearmen can keep you at range.

Yari Spear

Effective Against: Sickles & Kusarigama.

Strategy: Outranges fast-swinging chain weapons. Use heavy thrusts to keep agile foes at bay or push enemies off ledges.

Kusarigama

Effective Against: Shield Bearers & Crowds.

Strategy: Heavy chain attacks shatter shields instantly. Wide sweeping attacks provide great crowd control when swarmed.

Odachi

Effective Against: Brutes & Heavy Enemies.

Strategy: Massive two-handed katana that breaks heavy armor and staggers large enemies with high guard damage.

Color-Coded Defense System

No Glint (Standard Attack)

Hold L1 to block, or tap L1 right as the hit lands for a Perfect Parry to open up immediate counters.

Blue Glint (Heavy Attack)

Cannot be standard blocked. You must time a precision parry with L1 or dodge with Circle.

Red Glint (Unblockable Attack)

Cannot be blocked or parried. You must dodge out of the way using Circle.

Yellow Glint (Disarming Attack)

The enemy is attempting to knock your weapon away. Hold and release Triangle right as they strike to counter-disarm them, or dodge away.

Mastering quick weapon switching with R2 during battles is the key to surviving Ezo's hardest encounters!
