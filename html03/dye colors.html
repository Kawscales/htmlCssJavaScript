Dye Locations

White Dye

Location: Found in the Yōtei Grasslands, southeast part of the map.

Green Dye

Location: Found in Teshio Ridge along the Nikappu River near Kidaka Grove, northeastern part of the map.

Crimson Dye

Location: Found on the western coast of the Ishikari Plain. You must stoke and light the fire there to summon the merchant.

Black Dye

Location: Found on the western side of Teshio Ridge, unlocked after progressing through the Kitsune main questline.

Blue Dye

Location: Found on the southwestern edge of the Oshima Coast following the Otsuki River south (requires completing the main quests in the prior five regions to access).

I hope this guide helps you find all the dye locations!
